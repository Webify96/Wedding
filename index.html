Our Story

Two hearts, one journey.

From friendship to forever, our story has been written with faith, love and purpose. We can't wait to celebrate this new chapter with you.

We met on an ordinary day that turned out to be anything but. What started as a quiet friendship slowly grew into something neither of us could have planned, but something both of us are endlessly grateful for. Through seasons of change, laughter, and learning, we found in each other a steady kind of love: patient, honest, and rooted in faith.

Now, surrounded by the people who shaped us, we're ready to say "I do" and begin the next chapter — together. Thank you for being part of our story so far and for celebrating this next step with us.
